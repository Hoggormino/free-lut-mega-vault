Apple Log 2 / Apple Wide Gamut to Rec.709 - free LUT from Bransford Media

WHAT IT DOES
Turns Apple Log 2 / Apple Wide Gamut footage into normal-looking Rec.709 video. It uses the camera maker's published
Apple Log curve and Apple Wide Gamut gamut and the industry-standard ACES 2.0 display transform
(SDR, 100 nits, Rec.709), so skin, sky and highlights land where they should.

HOW TO USE IT
Apply it as the first step on your log clips, then adjust exposure and white balance before it
(or on the clip) rather than after it. Step-by-step guides for DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro,
Final Cut Pro, CapCut, LumaFusion and the Blackmagic Camera app:
https://bransfordmedia.com/luts/guides

Your editor should read the clip as video (legal) range, which is its default for camera files.
If blacks look lifted or crushed, check the clip's data-levels setting.
